# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-15

Until is a personal countdown calendar app that helps you view upcoming birthdays, anniversaries, and custom important dates.

## Data We Access

Until may request full access to your device calendar so it can:

- Read calendar events that look like birthdays or anniversaries.
- Create yearly recurring calendar events when you choose to sync a manually added countdown to Calendar.

The app also stores countdown event data locally so the main app and widget can show the same upcoming events.

Until may request notification permission only when you choose to enable a local reminder for an event. Local reminders are scheduled on your device.

## Data We Store

Until stores the following data locally on your device:

- Event title.
- Event date.
- Event category.
- Optional notes you enter.
- Local reminder preference.
- Related calendar event identifier, when an event is synced to Calendar.

This data is stored using platform-provided local storage. Where a widget or service card is available, the app uses the platform's local shared-storage mechanism to provide countdown data to that surface.

## Data We Do Not Collect

Until does not:

- Upload your calendar data to a server.
- Sell your data.
- Track you across apps or websites.
- Use third-party analytics SDKs.
- Use advertising SDKs.
- Require an account.

## Widget Data

Widgets and service cards read processed countdown data from platform-provided local shared storage. They do not directly access your system calendar.

## Calendar Permission

Calendar access is optional. If you deny calendar access, you can still use Until for local countdowns. Without full calendar access, the app cannot import system calendar events or sync new countdowns back to Calendar.

On iOS, you may hide an imported calendar event inside Until without deleting the original calendar event, and restore it later. Android and HarmonyOS do not provide this hide/restore feature; deletion choices distinguish removing the local app record from deleting the related calendar event.

## Notification Permission

Notification permission is optional. If you deny notification permission, you can still create, edit, and view countdown events. Until will not send local reminders without notification permission.

## Data Deletion

You can delete countdown events in the app. If the event was created in Calendar by Until, the app will try to remove future occurrences from Calendar. Local-only events are removed from local storage.

You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data.

## Children

Until is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not operate a server-side data collection system.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. Product and privacy changes should be reflected in `docs/product/PRODUCT_DOCUMENT.md` and this document.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, provide a support email or website before App Store submission.

# 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-07-15

待到是一款个人倒计时日历 App，用于查看即将到来的生日、纪念日和自定义重要日期。

## 我们访问的数据

当你使用日历导入或同步功能时，待到可能会请求日历权限，用于读取符合产品规则的系统日程，以及创建、更新或删除由待到同步的每年重复事件。

App 也会在本地保存倒计时事件数据，以便主 App 和小组件展示同一批即将到来的事件。

只有当你为事件开启本地提醒时，待到才会请求通知权限。本地提醒会在你的设备端排程。

## 我们保存的数据

待到会在你的设备本地保存以下数据：

- 事件名称。
- 事件日期。
- 事件分类。
- 你输入的可选备注。
- 本地提醒设置。
- 与系统日历同步时对应的日历事件标识符。

这些数据保存在平台提供的设备本地存储中。存在小组件或服务卡片时，App 使用平台提供的本地共享存储向其提供倒计时数据。

## 我们不会收集的数据

待到不会：

- 将你的日历数据上传到服务器。
- 出售你的数据。
- 跨 App 或网站追踪你。
- 使用第三方统计 SDK。
- 使用广告 SDK。
- 要求创建账号。

## 小组件数据

小组件和服务卡片从平台提供的本地共享存储中读取 App 已处理的倒计时数据，不会直接访问系统日历。

## 日历权限

日历权限是可选的。如果你拒绝日历权限，仍然可以使用本地倒计时功能，但无法把新倒计时同步到系统日历。

iOS 可在不删除系统日历原日程的情况下，在待到内隐藏导入事件并在后续恢复。Android 与 HarmonyOS 不提供隐藏/恢复功能；删除时会明确区分仅移除 App 本地记录和删除关联日历事件。

## 通知权限

通知权限是可选的。如果你拒绝通知权限，仍然可以创建、编辑和查看倒计时事件。没有通知权限时，待到不会发送本地提醒。

## 数据删除

你可以在 App 中删除倒计时事件。如果该事件由待到创建并同步到了系统日历，App 会尝试删除该日历事件的未来重复项。本地事件会从本地存储中移除。

你也可以从设备中删除 App，以移除本地保存的 App 数据。

## 儿童

待到不以收集儿童个人信息为目的。App 不运行服务器端数据收集系统。

## 变更

当 App 行为发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。产品和隐私相关变更应同步记录到 `docs/product/PRODUCT_DOCUMENT.md` 和本文档。

## 联系方式

提交 App Store 前，请补充支持邮箱或支持网站。
