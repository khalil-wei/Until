## 应用介绍

待到是一款简洁好看的倒计时日历应用，帮你记录生日、纪念日、节日、约定和每一个值得期待的日子。

你可以按生日、纪念日、自定义分类管理事件，支持公历和农历日期，适合记录农历生日、结婚纪念日、传统节日等重要日程。应用会自动计算距离下一次到来的天数，并按即将到来、生日、纪念日等维度清晰展示。

待到还支持本地提醒、可选的系统日历同步和桌面小组件。提醒可设为当天或提前 1、3、7 天，不依赖日历同步。界面克制、清爽，适合长期使用，不打扰，只在重要时刻提醒你。

## App Description

Until is a simple and elegant countdown calendar that helps you remember birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, promises, and every meaningful day ahead.

You can organize events by birthday, anniversary, or custom categories, with support for both Gregorian and lunar dates. It is ideal for lunar birthdays, wedding anniversaries, traditional holidays, and other recurring annual events. Until automatically calculates the next upcoming occurrence and presents your important days in a clear, calm timeline.

The app also supports optional device calendar sync, local reminders, and home screen widgets. Reminders can be scheduled for the same day or 1, 3, or 7 days in advance without requiring calendar sync. Designed to be quiet, focused, and pleasant to use every day.

## 功能要点

- 生日、纪念日、自定义日子管理
- 公历 / 农历日期支持
- 自动计算每年下一次到来时间
- 当天或提前 1/3/7 天的本地提醒
- 可选同步到系统日历
- 桌面小组件查看即将到来的日子
- 中英文界面支持
- 数据优先保存在本地

## Key Features

- Manage birthdays, anniversaries, and custom events
- Gregorian and lunar calendar support
- Automatic yearly countdown calculation
- Local reminders for the same day or 1, 3, or 7 days in advance
- Optional sync with device calendar
- Home screen widgets for upcoming days
- Chinese and English language support
- Local-first data storage

## 隐私说明摘要

待到仅在用户主动开启同步后访问系统日历，用于创建和维护由待到同步的日历事件。待到不读取其他来源的系统日程，也不上传用户日历、提醒或倒计时数据。
